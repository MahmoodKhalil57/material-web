# json-render catalog

Generated by `scripts/api/gen-json-render.mjs` from the public API; do not
edit. The catalog (`catalog.ts`) has one component per `md-gb-*` element
and `md-aria-tabpanel` (the panel of `md-gb-tab`), with its
JSON-serializable properties, slots and events, plus the `Text`
and `Html` primitives. `renderSpec()` (`renderer.ts`) renders specs of it;
`checkSpec()` (`check.ts`) validates them against the catalog and the M3
rules below.

## M3 rules

Checked by `checkM3()` and given to the generation prompt (`m3Prompt()`).
Each quote is checked against its spec page on every generation.

| Rule | Component | Severity | Spec |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Do not use NavDrawer (deprecated in M3 Expressive); use NavRail with expanded instead. | `NavDrawer` | warning | [components/navigation-drawer](https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-drawer): "The navigation drawer is no longer recommended in the Material 3 Expressive update" |
| A SegmentedButtonSet has 2 to 5 SegmentedButton children; use chips for more. | `SegmentedButtonSet` | error | [components/segmented-buttons](https://m3.material.io/components/segmented-buttons): "There can be anywhere from 2 to 5 segments in single-select and multi-select segmented buttons" |
| A NavBar has 3 to 5 NavBarItem destinations. | `NavBar` | error | [components/navigation-bar](https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-bar): "Navigation bars can have three to five destinations" |
| Show one Snackbar at a time; put several in a SnackbarHost, which queues them. | `Snackbar` | warning | [components/snackbar](https://m3.material.io/components/snackbar): "Only one snackbar may be displayed at a time." |
| A Dialog has at most two actions (buttons) in its "actions" slot. | `Dialog` | warning | [components/dialogs](https://m3.material.io/components/dialogs): "Dialogs should contain a maximum of two actions." |
| A FabMenu has up to six FabMenuItem children. | `FabMenu` | error | [components/fab-menu](https://m3.material.io/components/fab-menu): "The FAB menu can have up to six items" |
| Every FabMenuItem has label text (a Text child) next to its icon. | `FabMenuItem` | warning | [components/fab-menu](https://m3.material.io/components/fab-menu): "FAB menu items should always have label text." |
| Use at most one extended Fab (a Fab with a label) per screen. | `Fab` | warning | [components/extended-fab](https://m3.material.io/components/extended-fab): "Like the regular FAB, only one extended FAB should be used per screen." |
